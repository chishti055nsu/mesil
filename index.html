Microprocessor, Embedded Systems, and IoT Lab (MESIL)

Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE), School of Engineering & Physical Sciences (SEPS), North South University.

In the era of Industrial Revolution 4.0 (IR 4.0), Embedded Systems, Microprocessors, Internet of Things (IoT), and Blockchain technologies are the foundation of smart automation, healthcare, resilient cities, and cyber-physical security. MESIL serves as an academic and innovation hub bridging deep-tech research with real-world industry applications.
